    Contextual Help

    Getting Started

    Fill in the workflow header fields, add steps, and configure SLA targets for each priority level.

    Owner Role

    Receives escalations; pick the accountable senior role.

    Responsible Role

    The step's doer, not the owner.

    Expected Duration

    Working time to complete the step. Minutes, positive integers.

    Acknowledge

    Time to confirm intake; not the start of work.

    Start Work

    Time until the first step begins.

    Resolve

    Complete + published/updated in systems.

    Escalate After / To

    Safety net. After this time, responsibility passes to the selected role.

    Business Hours

    Feasibility uses this coverage (days, start–end, timezone).
